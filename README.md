
# 📊 Business Sales & Profit Dashboard Using Excel

## 📌 Overview

This project presents an interactive **Business Sales & Profit Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Excel**.

The project contains three dashboard sections:
- Sales Dashboard
- Profit Dashboard
- Data Insights

The dashboards provide insights into revenue, orders, customers, products, sales trends, profitability, and business performance to support data-driven decision-making.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

### 1. Sales Dashboard

(Sales Dashboard.png)

The Sales Dashboard provides an overview of business sales performance through key performance indicators and interactive visualizations.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Units Ordered
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value

**Key Visualizations:**
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Sales by City

**Key Insights:**
- Electronics contributes approximately 36% of sales in the displayed category chart.
- Running Shoes is the top-selling product in the displayed Top 10 Products chart.
- Hyderabad has the highest sales among the displayed cities.
- Sales decline noticeably from September to October and remain lower through the remaining months.

### 2. Profit Dashboard

(Profit Dashboard.png)

The Profit Dashboard focuses on profitability, profit margins, and product performance.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Average Profit per Order
- Profit per Unit
- Top Profitable Category

**Key Visualizations:**
- Monthly Profit Trend
- Profit by Category
- Top 10 Profitable Products
- Profit Margin by Category

**Key Insights:**
- Electronics is identified as the top profitable category in the displayed KPI.
- Beauty has the highest displayed category profit margin at approximately 56%.
- Fashion follows with a displayed profit margin of approximately 53%.
- Monthly profit declines noticeably around October.
- Running Shoes and Mechanical Keyboard appear among the leading profitable products.

### 3. Data Insights

(Data Insights.png)

The Data Insights section summarizes findings from the Sales and Profit dashboards and provides recommendations for improving business performance.

**Business Recommendations:**
- Focus on products with strong sales and healthy profit margins.
- Investigate monthly sales and profit declines.
- Review discounts, costs, and pricing to improve profitability.
- Analyze customer behavior to identify repeat-purchase opportunities.
- Compare category performance before planning promotional campaigns.
- Monitor revenue and profit together to support better decisions.

## ▶️ How to Use

1. Download or clone this GitHub repository.
2. Open `Business_Sales_Dashboard.xlsx` using Microsoft Excel.
3. Refresh PivotTables if required.
4. Use the Year, Category, City, and Order Status slicers to filter the dashboards.
5. Explore the Sales Dashboard, Profit Dashboard, and Data Insights sections.
6. Review the visualizations to understand business performance.
