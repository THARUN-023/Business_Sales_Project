
# 📊 Business Sales & Profit Dashboard Using Excel

## 📌 Overview

This project presents an interactive **Business Sales & Profit Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Excel**.

The project contains three dashboard sections:
- Sales Dashboard
- Profit Dashboard
- Data Insights

The dashboards help analyze revenue, orders, customers, products, monthly sales trends, profitability, and business performance to support data-driven decision-making.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

### 1. Sales Dashboard


![Sales Dashboard](./Sales%20Dashbaord.png)

The Sales Dashboard provides an overview of business sales performance through key performance indicators and interactive visualizations.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
- Total Revenue: ₹60,04,70,179
- Total Orders: 1,50,000
- Units Ordered: 4,49,319
- Total Customers: 59,640
- Average Order Value: ₹4,003

**Key Insights:**
- Electronics contributes approximately 36% of sales in the displayed category chart.
- Running Shoes is the top-selling product in the displayed chart.
- Hyderabad has the highest sales among the displayed cities.
- Sales decline noticeably from September to October and remain lower through December.

### 2. Profit Dashboard

![Profit Dashboard](Profit%20Dashboard.png)

The Profit Dashboard focuses on profitability, profit margins, and product performance.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
- Total Profit: 268,225,087.65
- Profit Margin: 44.7%
- Average Profit per Order: ₹1,788.17
- Profit per Unit: ₹597
- Top Profitable Category: Electronics

**Key Insights:**
- Electronics is identified as the top profitable category in the displayed KPI.
- Beauty has the highest displayed category profit margin at approximately 56%.
- Fashion follows with a displayed profit margin of approximately 53%.
- Monthly profit declines noticeably around October.
- Running Shoes and Mechanical Keyboard appear among the leading products in the profitability chart.

### 3. Data Insights

![Data Insights](Data%20insights.png)

This section presents business findings and recommendations based on the Sales and Profit dashboards.

**Business Recommendations:**
- Focus on products and categories with strong demand and healthy profit margins.
- Investigate monthly sales and profit declines using the dashboard filters.
- Review discounts, costs, and pricing to improve profitability.
- Analyze unique customers, repeat purchases, and average order value.
- Compare category-level performance before planning promotions.
- Investigate the October decline to identify potential causes.
